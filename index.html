WHAT IS HYPNOTHERAPY?

It's not magic.
It's science.

Hypnotherapy is a natural state of focused attention and deep relaxation. It helps you access your subconscious mind – the part that stores your beliefs, habits and emotions – so you can release what no longer serves you and create patterns that support your well-being.

No, you remain in complete control during the entire session. Hypnotherapy is a natural state of focused attention where you stay fully aware.

It is impossible to get stuck in hypnosis.
