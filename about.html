MY STORY

A Journey That Began With My Own Healing

I did not choose therapy. It chose me. In my mid-20s, I found myself caught in patterns I could not explain, anxiety that came from nowhere, and a constant feeling of not being enough.

My own healing process opened a door I did not know existed. Beneath the noise of my thoughts were old stories. Slowly, I learned to rewrite them.

Today my practice is built on one conviction. You are not broken. You are someone who has not been given the right tools yet.
